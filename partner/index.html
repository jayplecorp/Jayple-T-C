The Problem We Solve

Moving salon business details out of scattered channels.

Salons often coordinate pricing, available treatments, and bookings across fragmented paths—phone calls, direct messages, and offline notes. This manual approach can lead to scheduled double-bookings, miscommunicated durations, and limited local search visibility.

Jayple provides a unified space. By listing your business details on our platform, you give customers a clear structure to discover services and select open slots, simplifying the administrative steps before they walk through your doors.

Digital Discoverability

Provide clients with a clean web and mobile representation of your salon, listing operating hours, address, and styling images.

Catalog Organization

Display your complete list of grooming treatments, pricing variations, and time durations clearly, reducing catalog confusion.

Slot Coordination

Coordinate appointments through digital slot booking to manage flow and reduce manual call scheduling workload.

The Next Steps

Understanding the path to partnership.

Partnership begins with understanding your salon's context, services, and operational setup. In the next section, we outline how our review-led onboarding process operates.

Onboarding Process

Our review-led onboarding steps.

We focus on building a reliable ecosystem. Rather than offering unrestricted public registration, Jayple reviews each prospective partner business context and reputation to support platform quality.

Step 01

Express Interest

Provide your basic salon details, location, and business contact information by sending an email to our partner success team.

Step 02

Context Review

We review salon profiles, service standards, and local reputation as part of our onboarding review steps to ensure a positive customer experience.

Step 03

Discussion & Demo

Our team connects with you to arrange a discussion, explain the platform features, and answer operational questions.

Step 04

Activation & Launch

Approved salons complete account set up and catalog listing. We help verify that services display correctly before booking slots go live.

Platform Experience

Operational tools designed for salon management.

Jayple provides Salon Partners with a dedicated environment to manage listing details. The platform translates complex salon operations into simple, actionable updates.

Booking Slot Coordination

Salon partners can configure available daily hours, block out specific dates, and coordinate time slots to avoid client schedule overlaps.

Catalog Pricing & Service Listings

Update the prices of your styling treatments, modify descriptions, and list service durations to keep customers informed.

Operational Tracking

Review incoming customer requests, monitor active slots, and check scheduled timings from a unified partner dashboard.
